About the initiative

Accurate numbers are the first step to bringing every child back to the classroom. Data is captured where children live and rolled up level by level.

1

Identify

Collect actual OOSC from the Anganwadi Centre, the school and the habitation or catchment area through household surveys.

2

Profile

Count and profile children aged 6–18 (and below) who are not attending school, including CWSN.

3

Consolidate & review

Each level consolidates the data of the level below and reviews it with the concerned departments and partners.

Nine levels of reporting

How data flows

The same process is repeated in every district.

SurveyHabitation, AWC and school-level OOSC collection.
ComplexConsolidation of 10–12 schools and review with all HMs.
Mandal → DivisionMEO and DyEO consolidation with line departments and NGOs.
District → StateDEO/APC, Zone, State Nodal Officer, SPD and Director.

Field functionaries (CRMT / MEO / ALSCO) fill one proforma per child after each visit. Proforma-I is for children reported as already enrolled; Proforma-II is for never-enrolled and dropped-out children.
